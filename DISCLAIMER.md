2. **Estimates and generated plans can be wrong.** The Planner, readiness score, "What you can do," and Tonight's plan come from simplified models and fixed rules, with their assumptions shown. Real needs depend on your home, the weather, your health, your equipment, and how you use it. Treat every number and recommendation as a starting point, plan for more, and use your own judgment.

3. **Weather data.** Forecasts and alerts, where shown, come from the U.S. National Weather Service and may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Severity levels in WinterReady are your own setting, not official warnings. Always check official sources.

Use heaters, stoves, generators, and fuel only as their manufacturers direct and as local codes, fire rules, and your lease allow. Where WinterReady and those sources differ, follow those sources.
